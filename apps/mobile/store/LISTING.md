## Full description — en
Loopz remembers where you left off.

Follow shows, movies and anime in one place: mark the episodes you watched, see your progress per season, and know when the next episode airs before it does.

• Your library: everything you follow, by status — in progress, not started, finished.
• One-tap tracking: an episode, "up to here", or the whole season.
• Fast search for any title with posters and details.
• The same account as loopztv.com — what you watch here shows up there instantly.
• Arabic-first, with full English support.

Sign in with Google only — no new password to remember.
