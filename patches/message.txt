[deploy] D-1271 — a followed show learns about its new episode on Home

Library and Home decide "finished" from the aired count stored on the follow
row. It was refreshed only by opening the title page on the web, so a show
that was finished and then aired a new episode stayed "finished" for anyone
using the app alone (29 of 393 follows had a stored next date in the past).

Home now re-reads, before it is built, the follows whose stored next air date
has arrived and were not read since; and, inside its existing wave, the ones
with no next date that were not read for a week. Cap 12 per load.
