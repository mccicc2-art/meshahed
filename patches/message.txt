- 168 When IMDb and TMDB split an anime's seasons differently (Bleach TYBW: IMDb
  part 4, TMDB one season), the episode is matched by its air date from the
  official Kitsu API against the title's TMDB episodes; one episode or nothing.
- 168 One question per viewing: a request for an episode or movie answered less
  than an hour after its last request is the same viewing (Stremio repeats on
  resume/seek), so it is not asked again; after an hour's quiet it is asked
  again. Repeats already pending are closed as dismissed on Home.
