[deploy] D-1280 — Start / Pause / Resume and Remove in the long-press menu (migration 194)

Ahmed, 5 Oct, from a screenshot of the menu over a To watch title. The menu
rows now follow the title's state, in the app and in the web library:
- series not started: Start, Watched it all, Your comment, Remove
- series being watched: Pause, next episode (only where the card has no
  tick), Watched it all, Your comment, Red card
- series paused: Resume, Watched it all, Your comment, Red card
- movie not watched: Watched it, Your comment, Remove
Start puts a series in Continue Watching without marking an episode and takes
it out of To watch; Pause hides it from Continue Watching and keeps it on its
library shelf with a pause mark. Series only. Red card is for what was
started; Remove (with Undo) replaces it for what was not.

follows.watch_state ('started' | 'paused', migration 194, already applied)
carries the decision; a statement-level trigger on watched_episodes clears
'paused' when an episode is marked, and setDropped clears the state. The
status recipe (showStatusOf) reads it, so the library, smart lists and counts
agree. Continue Watching cards in the app now open the menu on long press.

Not in this batch: web Home posters and the web Continue card keep their old
menus. Not tried on a device before the push.
