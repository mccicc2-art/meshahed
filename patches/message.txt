[deploy] D-1281 — Pause moves a series to To watch instead of hiding it from Home

Ahmed tried D-1280 on Ted Lasso: Pause took it out of To watch as well. He
wants Pause to take a series out of Continue Watching only, and to see it in
To watch until Resume. D-1280 hid a paused series from both rows.

- To watch now lists every paused, unfinished series (with its pause mark and
  Resume in its menu), next to what is not started and what has a new season
  waiting. Start still moves a series to Continue Watching.
- Pause on a series with no episode watched is an undo of Start: the state is
  written empty, and watchStateOf() reads a stored 'paused' with no episode
  the same way, so its menu says Start again. One reader for Home, the
  library, the web library and the map sent to the app.
- The app's optimistic library patch follows the same rule.

No migration. Not tried on a device before the push.
