- 163 The addon accepts kitsu ids (Anime Kitsu catalog): kitsu:{id}:{ep} is mapped
  through the Kitsu addon's own meta (imdb_id, imdbSeason, imdbEpisode,
  absoluteEpisode) to IMDb, then TMDB; the episode is placed by its absolute
  number on TMDB's regular seasons (Bleach: IMDb S2E5 = TMDB S1E25), else by the
  IMDb numbering when TMDB holds it, else no signal. Manifest 1.3.0.
