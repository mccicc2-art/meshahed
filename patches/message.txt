[deploy] D-1277 — one bottom bar above the tabs instead of one per root

After D-1276 the bar icons still vanished for one or two frames on the first
visit to a tab (16 ms at Community, 33 ms at Discover, from 93 and 235):
each root drew its own bar, so a first visit mounted a new one and the images
arrive a frame after the mount. The tabs navigator now draws the bar once
(TabDock in tabBar) and each root registers what it used to pass to its bar
(DockLink): its press behaviour, its scroll-hide value and its leaving lock.
Same BottomNav, same look. Home keeps a seat of the bar height in its column.
Not tried on a device before the push.
